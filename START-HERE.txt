1. Double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
2. Drop your Excel file on the page. The app finds the heading row and columns by itself.
3. Set your start point, press Sort addresses, then Export Excel.
   The export is your own file, same layout and formatting, with rows in the new order.
To send addresses to other people, open the Distribute tab (Settings explains how to put the app online).
Move or copy this whole folder anywhere.
